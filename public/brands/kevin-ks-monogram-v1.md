# Kevin Song — KS monogram concept

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. This first concept pairs an
interlocking white K with a gold serif S on a transparent background. It is a
standalone asset for review; the existing website wordmark is retained.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE finished original KS monogram for Kevin Song,
a software builder and bilingual musician. Intended use: compact website
header logo and favicon on a near-black portfolio. Exact letters: K and S,
recognizable as both initials. Integrate a sturdy, angular, near-white K
(#FAFAFA) with an elegant curved warm-gold S (#D6B45E), interlocked into one
balanced compact silhouette. The S should evoke a modern editorial serif,
with subtle stroke contrast; the K feels precise and architectural. Flat crisp
solid shapes, clean intentional negative space, professional but expressive.
Prioritize recognition at 32px, broad enough strokes, limited complexity.
Square canvas; mark centered and occupying approximately 88% of canvas with
modest even padding. GENUINELY TRANSPARENT background. No extra text, no mockup,
no framing box, no badge, no decorative objects, no circuit patterns, no
musical notes, no shadows, no glow, no gradients, no 3D, no metallic texture,
no watermark. Deliver only the logo asset.
