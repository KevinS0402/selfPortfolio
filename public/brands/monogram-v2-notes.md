# Monogram variants — outline refinements

Both variants were edited with the built-in image-generation tool. They retain
transparent backgrounds and are used by the portfolio's theme-aware favicons.
The dark variant is also used in the shared website header.

## Dark variant prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Input image is the edit target, the existing
Kevin Song KS monogram. Remove the black/dark outline around the WHITE K.
K must be clean solid near-white #FAFAFA all the way to its outer edge, with
smooth white-to-transparent antialiasing. Remove any dark stroke, dark fringe,
dark shadow, or dark edge around the K, including where it crosses the gold S.
There must be NO BLACK OUTLINE anywhere around the white K. Keep the exact
existing K silhouette, serifs, position, size, overlap and alignment. Keep the
gold S and its exact existing color, shape, shading, scale and position.
Preserve original composition and canvas size. Clean stray speckles immediately
bordering the K to give a crisp edge. Transparent background with preserved
alpha, not a black or white backdrop. Do not redesign the monogram. Do not add
any objects, text, borders, glow or shadows. Deliver the same monogram, simply
with a clean unoutlined white K.

## Light variant prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Input image is the edit target: existing
light-mode Kevin Song KS monogram, with a black K and gold S. Change ONLY the
gold S by adding a thin, clean, consistent near-black (#09090B) outline around
its inner and outer silhouette. Approximately 12 pixels of outline on this
1254px canvas, enough definition for use as a favicon against white. Keep the
exact gold S shape, gold fill and existing shading, size, position, and its
overlap behind the K. Keep the black K exactly unchanged. At overlaps the K
stays in front. Preserve original monogram design, proportions, canvas size,
and alignment. Keep background genuinely transparent; do not add a white or
black backdrop. Crisp antialiased edges, no stray speckles. No shadow, glow,
new text, extra objects, frame or watermark. Deliver the existing logo with
only a thin black stroke on the gold S.
